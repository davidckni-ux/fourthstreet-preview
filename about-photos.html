About us

About 4th Street Mini Storage

Family-run since 2007

Owned and run by one family, not a chain. The person who answers the phone can make a decision.

Power in every unit

Every drive-up unit has its own outlet, and the power is part of the rent.

On 4th St, Vero Beach South

Easy to reach from US-1, with 24/7 access and camera surveillance.

4th Street Mini Storage is a family-owned storage facility on 4th St in Vero Beach South. Our family has owned and run it since 2007. Every unit is drive-up, every unit has its own outlet, and the power is included in the rent.

Built in 1978, looked after since

The buildings went up in 1978 and have been kept up ever since. They are plain and solid, and we keep them that way: doors that work and a property under camera surveillance.

Prices on the page

Our rates are on this website for anyone to see, the same for everyone, and the lease is month to month. Pick a size, see the number, and decide. If you have questions, call and a person will answer them.

Someone on the property

Jerry, our on-site manager, looks after the place and the people who store here. He will meet you at the property, show you a unit, and walk you through the lease. Tenants mention him by name in their reviews, and he has earned it.

The power

Most storage units are built without an outlet, and most leases say storage only. Ours have an outlet in every unit, and we include the power in the rent. That one detail is why contractors charge their tools here overnight, why boat owners keep a battery tender on the trailer, and why small businesses run their inventory out of a unit instead of a warehouse.

Where we are

725 4th St, Vero Beach, FL 32962, in Vero Beach South. Our tenants come from across Vero Beach, Sebastian, Fellsmere, Wabasso and Gifford, and from anyone passing through Indian River County who needs a drive-up unit with power.

Good to know

Can I see a unit before I rent?

Yes. Call (772) 778-1210 during our hours, Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and our on-site manager will meet you at the property.

Where are you?

725 4th St, Vero Beach, FL 32962, in Vero Beach South. Map and directions.

Do I need to sign a long lease?

No. Everything is month to month. Stay a month or stay for years.

Is it climate controlled?

No. Our units are drive-up and not climate controlled. For tools, equipment, vehicles, boats, inventory and most household items that is the right fit. If you are storing antiques, photographs or electronics for a long time, a climate-controlled unit elsewhere will serve you better, and we will tell you so.
