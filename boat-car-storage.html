What fits

Cars and SUVs

A 12x20 is 12 feet wide and 20 feet deep. Most cars and SUVs fit with room to open a door.

Boats on trailers

Many trailered boats fit. Give us the overall trailer length and width and we will say yes or no.

Motorcycles

A 10x10 holds a motorcycle and the gear, with the battery on a tender.

Why the outlet matters for a vehicle

A battery tender or trickle charger plugged in beside the vehicle. No dead battery after a month away.

The outlet is included in the rent. Nothing to arrange, nothing extra on the bill.

A few rules

  • Battery on a tender or disconnected
  • Keep fuel to a minimum
  • Nothing leaking on the floor; use a drip pan
  • Storage and charging only, no mechanical work in the unit
  • Vehicles stay inside the unit, never in the lane

If you are not sure about something, ask.

Vehicle questions

Will my car or boat fit in a 12x20?

A 12x20 is 12 feet wide and 20 feet deep, 240 square feet. Cars, SUVs and many trailered boats fit. Measure the overall length and width of the vehicle or trailer, call (772) 778-1210, and we will tell you straight.

Can I park in the lane?

The lanes between the buildings are shared. Pull up to your door to load and unload, then move your vehicle so your neighbors can reach their units. A vehicle that stays on the property goes inside a unit, not in the lane.

Can I plug things in?

Yes. Every unit has its own outlet, and power is included in the rent. Lights, battery chargers, power tools and a battery tender on a boat or car are all fine.

Can I work on my car or run a shop out of the unit?

Units are for storage and charging only. Charging batteries and tools, and keeping a tender on a vehicle, is fine.

When can I get to my unit?

Any time. Tenant access is 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. There is no gate; the property is under camera surveillance. For calls and visits, our hours are Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
