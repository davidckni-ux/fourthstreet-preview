Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about power, sizes, access and the lease. If your question is not here, call (772) 778-1210 and ask.

Power in the unit

Can I plug things in?

Yes. Every unit has its own outlet, and power is included in the rent. Lights, battery chargers, power tools and a battery tender on a boat or car are all fine.

Is the power metered or billed separately?

No. Power is part of the rent. There is no meter on your unit and no separate electric bill.

Can I work on my car or run a shop out of the unit?

Units are for storage and charging only. Charging batteries and tools, and keeping a tender on a vehicle, is fine.

Sizes and what fits

What sizes do you have?

Two drive-up sizes, both with an outlet inside: a 12x20 (240 square feet, roll-up door) from $289 a month and a 10x10 (100 square feet) from $149 a month. Power is included in both. See sizes and prices.

Is it climate controlled?

No. Our units are drive-up and not climate controlled. For tools, equipment, vehicles, boats, inventory and most household items that is the right fit. If you are storing antiques, photographs or electronics for a long time, a climate-controlled unit elsewhere will serve you better, and we will tell you so.

Will my car or boat fit in a 12x20?

A 12x20 is 12 feet wide and 20 feet deep, 240 square feet. Cars, SUVs and many trailered boats fit. Measure the overall length and width of the vehicle or trailer, call (772) 778-1210, and we will tell you straight.

Access and the property

When can I get to my unit?

Any time. Tenant access is 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. There is no gate; the property is under camera surveillance. For calls and visits, our hours are Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Is the property gated?

No. There is no gate and no code to punch in. Tenant access is 24/7, the property is under camera surveillance, and there is an on-site manager.

Can I see a unit before I rent?

Yes. Call (772) 778-1210 during our hours, Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and our on-site manager will meet you at the property.

Where are you?

725 4th St, Vero Beach, FL 32962, in Vero Beach South. Map and directions.

Can I park in the lane?

The lanes between the buildings are shared. Pull up to your door to load and unload, then move your vehicle so your neighbors can reach their units. A vehicle that stays on the property goes inside a unit, not in the lane.

Lease, payments and fees

Do I need to sign a long lease?

No. Everything is month to month. Stay a month or stay for years.

Can my business rent a unit?

Yes. Month to month with a receipt for every payment, so the unit goes on the books like any other expense.

Is there a move-in fee?

No. There is no admin or move-in fee. The pricing page lists everything you pay.

Ready to see a unit?

Call and our on-site manager will meet you at the property. Or reserve online and we will follow up by phone.
