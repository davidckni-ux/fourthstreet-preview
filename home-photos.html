Good to know

Common questions

Can I plug things in?

Yes. Every unit has its own outlet, and power is included in the rent. Lights, battery chargers, power tools and a battery tender on a boat or car are all fine.

Is it climate controlled?

No. Our units are drive-up and not climate controlled. For tools, equipment, vehicles, boats, inventory and most household items that is the right fit. If you are storing antiques, photographs or electronics for a long time, a climate-controlled unit elsewhere will serve you better, and we will tell you so.

When can I get to my unit?

Any time. Tenant access is 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. There is no gate; the property is under camera surveillance. For calls and visits, our hours are Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Can I see a unit before I rent?

Yes. Call (772) 778-1210 during our hours, Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and our on-site manager will meet you at the property.
